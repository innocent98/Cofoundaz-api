Before you commit months to building, run one cheap test to see whether people want the result you promise.

## Three quick demand tests

- **Smoke test page.** Publish a one page description of the product with a clear call to action, such as "Join the waitlist" or "Book a demo". Send real traffic from the places where your customers gather. Measure how many take the action.
- **Manual first.** Offer the service by hand to five customers and see whether they come back for a second time.
- **Pre-order.** Ask for a small deposit for early access and track who pays.

## Set a bar before you start

Choose a target number in advance so that you cannot argue yourself into a pass. For example: *"At least 30 of the first 300 visitors leave their WhatsApp number, or 5 of 20 contacted prospects book a call."* The exact numbers depend on your market. What matters is writing the bar down first.

## Read the result

- **Above the bar:** build the core flow from the previous lessons, and keep the same people close as your first testers.
- **Near the bar:** change one thing, such as the headline or the audience, and repeat.
- **Far below the bar:** go back to your interview notes. The problem or the audience may need to change.

## Set a finish line

Before you start building, choose a ship date and a definition of done: what the customer can do, and how you will know. A date keeps scope honest. Aim to put the core flow in front of real customers within weeks, not months.
