Most early ideas fail because the problem was never sharp, not because the product was weak. In this course you turn a loose idea into a one-sentence problem statement, find the people who feel the problem most, and size the pain so you know whether it is worth a year of your life.

You need no prototype and no money for this course. A notebook and a few honest conversations are enough.
