Pricing feels like a number you pick at the end. It is better treated as an experiment you start early. A price tells you who your customer is, what you must deliver and whether the business can work.

This course gives you a method for a first price in naira, a way to choose a model that fits your product, and a small test that uses real money instead of opinions.
