The same price can be packaged in different ways. The model you pick shapes how customers behave and how steady your income is.

## Common models

- **Subscription (monthly or yearly):** predictable income, good when customers use the product continuously, like accounting or booking tools.
- **Usage based:** customers pay per transaction, message or delivery. It lowers the barrier to starting, and income grows with their success, but it is less predictable.
- **One-off or project fee:** fits services and custom work. It is simple to sell, but you must keep finding new customers.
- **Tiers:** two or three plans with clear differences. Useful once you know which features different customers value.

## How to choose

Ask three questions:

1. **How does the customer get value?** Continuous value suggests subscription. Value per event suggests usage.
2. **How do they like to pay?** Many Nigerian small businesses prefer to pay small amounts often, by bank transfer or card, over a big annual payment. A monthly plan with an annual discount can serve both.
3. **How do you want to grow?** Subscriptions reward retention. Usage rewards volume.

## Keep it simple at first

Start with one plan and one price. Add tiers only after real customers show you where they differ. Avoid free plans until you know what a paying customer looks like, because a free plan can fill your support inbox without testing demand.

Write down your model, your price and the one sentence you will use to explain it.
