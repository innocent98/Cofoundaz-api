Do not guess which channel will work. Test three, in small batches, side by side.

## Choose candidates

Pick three channels that fit your customers. Examples: referrals, WhatsApp or Instagram content, partnerships, cold outreach by email or phone, local events, paid ads, or search content. Include at least one you have not tried.

## Design each test

For every channel, write down before you start:

- **Budget:** time and money you will spend, for example ₦30,000 and ten hours over two weeks
- **Action:** exactly what you will do
- **Measure:** the number that counts, such as paying customers, not clicks
- **Bar:** the result that would make the channel worth continuing

## Keep tests comparable

Run the tests in the same period, with a similar budget and the same offer. If one channel gets your best energy and the others get leftovers, the result is meaningless.

## Track from first touch to payment

Give each channel a way to be recognised: a unique link, a code word or a question at signup, "How did you hear about us?". Without it, you will credit the wrong channel.

## Be patient with noise

A channel with one great week and three dead ones has not proven itself. Look for results that repeat. A smaller but steady channel often beats a spike.

At the end of two weeks you should have a small table: channel, spend, customers won, cost per customer. That table decides where the next phase of effort goes.
