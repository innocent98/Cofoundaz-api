Customer conversations are the cheapest research a founder can do and the one most founders do badly. They pitch instead of listen, or they collect compliments that mean nothing.

This course gives you a repeatable method: find the right people, ask about what they actually did, and turn the notes into decisions. It works for B2B and consumer products, in person, by phone or on WhatsApp calls.
