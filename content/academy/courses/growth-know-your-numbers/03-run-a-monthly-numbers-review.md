A short, regular review turns numbers from a surprise into a habit.

## Pick a fixed time

Choose one hour at the same point each month, for example the first Monday. Invite everyone who influences spending or sales. Keep the meeting short and the page simple.

## The one page to bring

Put the same eight figures on a single page every month:

1. Cash in the bank
2. Net burn
3. Runway in months
4. Revenue this month and last month
5. New customers and lost customers
6. Contribution margin per unit
7. Customer acquisition cost by channel
8. Money owed to you and money you owe

Compare each number to last month and to the plan.

## Ask four questions

- What changed, and why?
- What is the biggest risk in the next 90 days?
- Which cost can we cut without hurting customers?
- What is the single number we most want to move next month?

## Write down decisions

End with at most three decisions, each with an owner and a date. A review without decisions is only a report.

## Keep the books clean

Record income and expenses weekly, keep receipts and separate business and personal money. Clean books make the review quick and make fundraising, tax and audits far less painful.
