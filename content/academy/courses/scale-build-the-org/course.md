Growth changes what a founder's job is. Tasks that used to live in your head must live in documents, and decisions must move to other people.

This course covers three foundations of a larger team: documented processes, a hiring plan and clear terms for roles and equity. It is practical guidance, not legal advice, so talk to a lawyer before signing anything.
