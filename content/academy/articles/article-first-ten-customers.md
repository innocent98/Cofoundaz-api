Ads and big campaigns can work later. The first ten customers almost always come from direct conversations.

## Why conversations beat ads at the start

- You learn why people say yes or no, which an ad click never tells you.
- You can change the offer the same day.
- It costs time, not a marketing budget, which most early founders do not have.

## A weekly routine

Aim for 15 to 25 meaningful conversations a week.

1. **Make a list** of 50 people who fit your customer description.
2. **Send a short, personal message** that mentions their situation and asks one question.
3. **Follow up** two or three days later. Many replies arrive on the second message.
4. **Offer a clear next step**, such as a 15 minute call or a trial.
5. **Ask for an introduction** at the end of every call, whether they buy or not.

## What to say

Lead with the problem, not the features. *"I help salon owners stop losing bookings in WhatsApp chats. Could I show you how in 15 minutes?"* is clearer than a paragraph on technology.

## Track in a simple table

Name, date, channel, status and next step. Review it every Friday. If many people reply but few buy, the offer needs work. If few reply, the message or audience needs work.

## Make the first customers special

Give them extra attention, ask for honest feedback and thank them in public with permission. They will give you the stories that win the next hundred customers.
