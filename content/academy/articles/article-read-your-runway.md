Runway tells you how many months you can keep operating if nothing changes. You can calculate it in five minutes.

## The three figures

1. **Cash today.** The total of your bank, mobile money and cash on hand.
2. **Monthly spending.** Everything you pay out in a typical month.
3. **Monthly money in.** What customers actually pay you, not what they have promised.

## The calculation

Net burn is spending minus money in. Runway is cash divided by net burn.

*Cash ₦3,000,000. Spending ₦900,000. Money in ₦300,000. Net burn ₦600,000. Runway 5 months.*

## Make it realistic

- Add costs that do not occur monthly, such as annual subscriptions, tax payments and equipment.
- Leave out late or uncertain customer payments until they arrive.
- Run a cautious case where revenue falls by 20 percent, and a good case where it rises. Keep both numbers.

## What the number should trigger

- **More than 9 months:** you can focus on growth, with a monthly check.
- **6 to 9 months:** start planning your next step, whether revenue growth or fundraising.
- **Under 6 months:** act now. Cut non essential costs, collect what is owed and talk to people who might invest or lend, before urgency weakens your position.

## A weekly habit

Every Friday, update the three figures and write the runway on the first page of your team notes. Seeing the number each week turns a vague worry into a plan.
