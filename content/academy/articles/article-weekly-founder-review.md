Founders live in the urgent. A fixed weekly review keeps you pointed at what matters.

## When and where

Pick a quiet slot, such as Friday afternoon or Sunday evening. Thirty minutes, a notebook or a document, and no phone.

## The five steps

1. **Look back (5 min).** What did you finish? What did you plan and not finish?
2. **Check the numbers (5 min).** Cash and runway, new customers, revenue, and the one number your business cares most about this month.
3. **Listen to customers (5 min).** What did customers say or do this week? Any complaint that appeared twice?
4. **Find the bottleneck (5 min).** What single thing, if fixed, would help most?
5. **Plan next week (10 min).** Choose three outcomes, not twenty tasks. Put the most important one in the first slot of Monday.

## Keep it honest

Write down what did not work without blaming yourself. A review that turns into guilt gets skipped. Ask "what will I change?" and move on.

## Share with your team

If you work with others, share the outcomes of the week in a short message. A team that sees the same three priorities pulls in the same direction.

## Use your tools

The Mission page in Cofoundaz can hold your top tasks, and the Health Score shows if something is slipping. Use them as prompts during the review.

After four weeks you will have a record of your decisions, which is useful when you plan a quarter or talk to investors.
