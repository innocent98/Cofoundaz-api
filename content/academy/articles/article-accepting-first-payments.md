You do not need a full payment system to charge your first customers. You need a clear way to ask for money and a record of what came in.

## Start simple

- **Bank transfer** to a business account, with a clear reference. It is the easiest option and well trusted by many customers.
- **Payment links** from a provider such as Paystack or Flutterwave. You send a link, the customer pays by card or transfer, and the provider records it.
- **Invoices** for business customers, with a due date and your account details.

Cofoundaz lets you connect your own provider account in the Finance Hub, so payments appear next to your other numbers. You choose the provider that suits your customers.

## Keep your money separate

Open a business account as soon as you can and keep personal and business money apart. It makes your records cleaner and builds trust with customers and later with investors.

## Record every payment

For each payment, note the date, customer, amount, method and what it was for. A weekly ten minute habit prevents a messy year end.

## Watch the fees

Providers charge a percentage and sometimes a fixed fee per transaction. Add this to your cost per sale so that your margin is real.

## Think about refunds and failed payments

Write a short rule for refunds and a message to send when a payment fails. Customers remember how you handle problems.

## When to upgrade

Add subscriptions, automated reminders and reconciliation only when you are handling enough payments that doing it by hand starts to cost you time or cause mistakes.
